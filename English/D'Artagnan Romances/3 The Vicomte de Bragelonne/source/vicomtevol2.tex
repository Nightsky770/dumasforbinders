\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\textit{The Vicomte of Bragelonne} was serialised between 1847 and 1850.  As wtih most works by Alexandre Dumas \textit{père} (1802--1870), it was a collaboration with his writing partner Auguste Maquet (1813--1888).

Illustrations by Malcolm Patterson (1873--1941) are from an edition published by Collins' Clear-Type Press in Glasgow and London (UK).

\end{minipage}
